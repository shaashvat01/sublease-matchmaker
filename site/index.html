How it works

One DM. Your agent does the rest.

  1. 1

    Tell your agent

    DM the bot in plain words. It asks one question at a time, including your private limit: the lowest rent you'd take, or the most you can pay.

  2. 2

    Get matched

    Listings are ranked by how much of your dates they cover, how they fit your budget, and minutes to your office.

  3. 3

    Agents negotiate

    Your agent and theirs trade offers and counters, prorate partial stays and nudge dates. A referee checks every move.

  4. 4

    You both confirm

    Nothing is final until both students tap Confirm deal. Either of you can walk away and nothing is shared.

  5. 5

    Meet in a private thread

    Only then do you see names and the full address, in a thread just for the two of you, with a safety checklist.

    The referee

    A referee reads every message before the other side does.

    Agents are persuasive. That's why neither one talks to the other directly.

    Claude Sonnet referee

    Catches indirect hints at a private limit, pressure tactics and anything that would break fair-housing rules, then rewrites the message. When the agents stall, it suggests a compromise that works for both.

    Hard rules in code, underneath

    No offer can cross its student's limit, whatever a model says. Private numbers are redacted, out-of-limit accepts are blocked, and rounds are capped.

    Listings can't filter by race, religion, sex, national origin, disability or family status. Lifestyle rules are fine.

    FAQ

    Good questions.

    Is my lowest rent or max budget really private?

    Yes. It lives only with your own agent. The referee checks every message for hints, and code blocks any number that slips through. The other side only ever sees offers.

    Who can see the negotiation?

    The server sees the moves on #negotiation-log without names or addresses. Your profile and limits stay in your DMs.

    What if our dates only partly overlap?

    The agents prorate the rent by day and can nudge move-in or move-out within how flexible each of you said you are.

    Can I say no after the agents agree?

    Always. Nothing is final until both of you tap Confirm, and declining shares nothing.

    Does any money go through the bot?

    No. You pay each other after touring the place and signing a written sublease.

    Which campuses does it work for?

    Any campus community on Discord. The first listings are around Tempe, Phoenix, Scottsdale, Mesa, Chandler and Gilbert.
